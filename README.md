### 🛡️ About Me

> *"Defending the digital frontier, one log at a time."*

I am an **Information Security undergraduate student** and **Level 2 Technical Support Analyst** with hands-on experience in **Identity & Access Management (Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, CyberArk PAM)**. 

I am actively bridging my strong IT operations and infrastructure background into full-time **Defensive Security (Blue Team / SOC)** and **Threat Hunting**. My daily routine involves simulating real-world adversary attack paths, investigating telemetry across cloud and endpoint environments, developing detection rules (KQL / Sigma), and publishing security write-ups.

- 🎯 **Target Roles:** Security Operations Analyst (SOC Tier 1 / Tier 2) | Threat Hunter | IAM & Cloud Security Specialist
- 🔭 **Current Focus:** SIEM Detection Engineering (Microsoft Sentinel & Splunk), Endpoint Telemetry (Microsoft Defender XDR & Wazuh), Network Forensics.
- 📚 **In Progress:** Preparing for **Microsoft SC-200** (Security Operations Analyst) and **CompTIA CySA+** (Cybersecurity Analyst).
